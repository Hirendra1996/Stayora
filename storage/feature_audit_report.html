Strategic Implementation Roadmap

Step-by-step roadmap to achieve 100% marketplace capability
Phase 1 • Core MVP Hardening (1-2 Weeks)

Booking & Payment Completeness

Fill critical gaps in the existing checkout and verification workflow.

  • ✔
    In-App Payment Proof Upload: Add UTR / Transaction ID input and screenshot upload directly in the booking modal instead of forcing WhatsApp.
  • ✔
    Separate Payment Status: Add payment_status ('unpaid','partial','paid','refunded') to booking_requests.
  • ✔
    15-Minute Booking Hold: Prevent simultaneous bookings with automated hold expiry timestamp.
  • ✔
    PDF Booking Voucher: Auto-generate booking confirmation PDF with QR code and check-in directions.
  • ✔
    Offline Booking Ledger: Form for owners/admins to record phone bookings with guest details.
Phase 2 • Marketplace Growth (2-3 Weeks)

Trust, Pricing & Owner Power

Empower owners and build customer confidence.

  • ★
    Guest Reviews & Ratings: Post-checkout verified reviews with 5 rating stars and admin moderation.
  • ★
    Dynamic Pricing Engine: Weekend multipliers (Friday/Saturday) and custom holiday dates.
  • ★
    Owner Inquiry Inbox: Allow owners to view and respond to leads for their own properties.
  • ★
    Owner Financials & Earnings: Earnings tab with net payout calculation and commission tracking.
  • ★
    Coupon Engine: Welcome coupons and percentage discounts with minimum booking totals.
Phase 3 • Enterprise Scale (Post Launch)

Automation & Operations

Enterprise features for multi-city scaling across India.

  • ⚡
    Online Payment Gateway: Razorpay / Cashfree integration for instant automated confirmation.
  • ⚡
    Automated WhatsApp Notifications: Interakt / Gupshup WhatsApp API triggers on booking events.
  • ⚡
    Multi-Admin RBAC: Role permissions for Finance Admin, Property Manager, Support Team.
  • ⚡
    Dynamic CMS & Blog: Admin managed destinations guide, travel blogs, and local SEO pages.
  • ⚡
    Mobile App (React Native / Flutter): Dedicated Host app and Guest app.
